Three phases. One survivor group.

Every round follows the same loop, just like the game, only now you can't hide behind a screen.

  1. Suit up

    Check in, get your colour and a task list, and learn the ship layout during the briefing.

  2. Work the ship

    Complete tasks around campus. Impostors sabotage and eliminate while staying unnoticed.

  3. Call the vote

    Found a body or a suspect? Call a meeting, share what you saw, argue your case, and vote.

Quick answers

Do I need to know Among Us?

No. The briefing covers everything you need, and the game is easier to learn by playing.

Can I join with friends?

Yes, register separately and tell us who you'd like to be near. Roles are still random.

What should I bring?

Comfortable shoes, a water bottle, and your student ID.

Who is organising this?

SPARK, the Society for Programming, Automation, Research & Knowledge at IIIT Bhopal. Find us on LinkedIn.
